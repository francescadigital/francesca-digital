# Francesca Digital — Content Strategy

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Francesca Digital communicates.

It is the single source of truth for every piece of written content across the website, portfolio, documentation and future marketing materials.

Every sentence should support the brand positioning established in Brand Foundation.

---

# Communication Goal

The website should make visitors think:

> "These people understand the problem before talking about the solution."

The objective is not to impress.

The objective is to build confidence.

---

# Tone of Voice

The voice of Francesca Digital is:

- Calm
- Clear
- Precise
- Professional
- Thoughtful
- Confident
- Engineering-oriented

Never:

- loud
- aggressive
- sales-heavy
- exaggerated
- trendy
- overly emotional

---

# Personality

Imagine a senior engineer explaining a complex problem to a CEO.

Not a marketer.

Not a salesperson.

Not a startup founder trying to impress investors.

---

# Writing Principles

Every paragraph should optimize for:

1. Clarity
2. Simplicity
3. Precision
4. Meaning
5. Confidence

If a sentence does not increase understanding,
remove it.

---

# Messaging Priorities

Every page should reinforce these ideas:

- Digital products should solve business problems.
- Simplicity creates quality.
- Good architecture reduces future cost.
- Design supports usability.
- Performance is a feature.
- Long-term maintainability matters.

---

# Language Rules

Prefer:

Build

Design

Develop

Improve

Refine

Simplify

Structure

Deliver

Avoid:

Leverage

Innovative

Revolutionary

Game-changing

World-class

Cutting-edge

Best

Amazing

Powerful solutions

---

# Headlines

Headlines should be:

Short.

Specific.

Confident.

Examples:

Design with clarity.

Digital products built with intent.

Every detail has a purpose.

Build less.
Achieve more.

---

# Paragraphs

Prefer:

Short paragraphs.

One idea per paragraph.

Avoid walls of text.

---

# CTA Principles

CTA buttons should sound like actions.

Preferred:

Start a project

Discuss your goals

View selected work

See the process

Avoid:

Contact us

Submit

Learn more

Click here

---

# Service Pages

Every service follows the same structure.

1. Problem

2. Solution

3. Process

4. Deliverables

5. Result

6. CTA

---

# Portfolio Pages

Every project follows:

Context

Challenge

Approach

Design

Development

Outcome

Lessons

---

# Contact Page

The visitor should know:

What to write.

What happens next.

How long the response takes.

Who will answer.

---

# Hero Formula

Hero always answers four questions.

1.

What do we do?

2.

Who is it for?

3.

Why are we different?

4.

What should the visitor do next?

---

# Editing Checklist

Before publishing any content ask:

Is it shorter?

Is it clearer?

Can one sentence be removed?

Is every claim believable?

Would a senior client trust this wording?

If the answer is "no",

rewrite it.

---

# Final Principle

Do not try to sound impressive.

Try to be understandable.

Confidence comes from clarity.
