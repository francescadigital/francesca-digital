# Hero Design Specification

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the visual architecture of the Hero section.

It is implementation-independent.

Developers should be able to recreate the Hero without making design decisions.

---

# Layout

Desktop

Two-column layout.

Left column

≈55%

Right column

≈45%

Both columns are vertically centered.

Hero occupies approximately one viewport height.

---

# Left Column

Order:

1. Eyebrow

2. Headline

3. Supporting paragraph

4. CTA group

5. Trust signals

---

# Eyebrow

Small uppercase label.

Letter spacing is generous.

Accent color.

Purpose:

Introduce positioning.

Examples:

Independent Digital Studio

Strategy • Design • Engineering

---

# Headline

Maximum:

2 lines.

Large.

High contrast.

Very tight tracking.

Weight:

Medium.

Never bold.

---

# Supporting Paragraph

Maximum width:

~38 characters.

Comfortable reading width.

Muted color.

Two short paragraphs are preferred over one long paragraph.

---

# CTA Group

Horizontal layout.

Primary button first.

Secondary button second.

Gap:

16–20px.

---

# Trust Signals

Exactly three.

Displayed horizontally.

Separated visually.

No icons.

Simple typography.

Examples:

Performance-first

Accessible by default

Maintainable architecture

---

# Right Column

Purpose:

Create brand recognition.

Not illustrate a product.

Not show devices.

Not show people.

Not use stock images.

---

# Blueprint Panel

Contains:

Logo mark.

Geometric composition.

Subtle grid.

Three disciplines.

Three principles.

Everything aligned.

Everything intentional.

---

# Visual Style

Dark.

Minimal.

Editorial.

Architectural.

Engineering aesthetic.

---

# Borders

Thin.

Low contrast.

Used to organize information.

Never decorative.

---

# Motion

Fade.

Slide.

Opacity.

Scale.

Maximum duration:

400ms.

Motion should reinforce hierarchy.

Never entertain.

---

# Mobile Layout

Single column.

Visual panel moves below CTA.

Spacing increases.

Nothing overlaps.

Buttons become full width.

Trust signals become vertical.

---

# Accessibility

Keyboard navigable.

Reduced motion supported.

Contrast AA minimum.

No information conveyed by color alone.

---

# Success Criteria

A visitor should understand within five seconds:

Who we are.

What we build.

How we think.

What to do next.

Without scrolling.

---

# Final Principle

The Hero should feel engineered.

Not decorated.
