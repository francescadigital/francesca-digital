# Process Experience

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The process should remove uncertainty.

Clients should understand exactly what happens after they decide to work with Francesca Digital.

The process is not about internal production.

It is about the client experience.

---

# Main Principle

Clients should never wonder:

"What happens next?"

Every stage should answer that question before it appears.

---

# Process Philosophy

A successful process is:

Predictable.

Transparent.

Collaborative.

Focused.

Calm.

Professional.

---

# Client Journey

## 01

Initial Conversation

Purpose

Understand the business.

Understand the goals.

Understand the project.

Client Outcome

Confidence that the project is understood before any work begins.

---

## 02

Strategy & Planning

Purpose

Define structure.

Define priorities.

Define technical direction.

Client Outcome

A clear roadmap with no unnecessary uncertainty.

---

## 03

Design & Validation

Purpose

Transform strategy into interfaces.

Validate important decisions before development.

Client Outcome

Visibility into the future product before it is built.

---

## 04

Development

Purpose

Build the product with performance, accessibility and maintainability as priorities.

Client Outcome

Reliable implementation without surprises.

---

## 05

Launch

Purpose

Deploy.

Verify.

Optimize.

Client Outcome

A confident release.

---

## 06

Long-term Support

Purpose

Improve.

Maintain.

Refine.

Client Outcome

A website that continues to perform after launch.

---

# Communication Rules

Every stage explains:

What happens.

Why it matters.

What the client receives.

---

# Tone

Professional.

Calm.

Transparent.

No marketing language.

No exaggerated promises.

---

# Final Principle

The process should reduce anxiety.

Not increase it.
