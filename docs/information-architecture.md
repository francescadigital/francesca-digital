# Information Architecture

> Structure of the Francesca Digital platform.

---

# Product Structure

The platform consists of several primary sections designed to guide visitors from discovery to contact.

```
Home
│
├── Services
│
├── Projects
│
├── About
│
├── Blog
│
└── Contact
```

---

# Home

Purpose:

Introduce the brand, communicate value and guide visitors toward relevant actions.

Main sections:

- Hero
- Services Overview
- Featured Projects
- Development Process
- Why Choose Francesca Digital
- Testimonials (future)
- Latest Articles (future)
- Contact CTA
- Footer

---

# Services

Purpose:

Present available services and explain how they solve business problems.

Potential services:

- Business Websites
- Corporate Websites
- Landing Pages
- UI/UX Design
- Frontend Development
- Website Redesign
- Technical Consulting

---

# Projects

Purpose:

Showcase portfolio case studies with a focus on process and outcomes rather than screenshots alone.

Each case study should include:

- Background
- Challenge
- Solution
- Design Process
- Technology
- Results
- Gallery

---

# About

Purpose:

Build trust by explaining the philosophy, process and values behind Francesca Digital.

Potential sections:

- Story
- Mission
- Values
- Workflow
- Technologies
- Frequently Asked Questions

---

# Blog

Purpose:

Share knowledge, improve SEO and demonstrate expertise.

Categories may include:

- Web Development
- UI/UX
- Performance
- Accessibility
- SEO
- Business
- Case Studies

---

# Contact

Purpose:

Provide a clear and simple way for potential clients to start a conversation.

Potential elements:

- Contact Form
- Email
- Project Brief
- Frequently Asked Questions

---

# Global Navigation

Primary navigation:

- Home
- Services
- Projects
- About
- Blog
- Contact

---

# Footer

The footer should contain:

- Navigation
- Contact Information
- Social Links
- Copyright
- Legal Links

---

# Future Expansion

The architecture should support future additions such as:

- Multi-language support
- CMS integration
- Client Portal
- Design System Documentation
- Careers
- Open Source
- Resources

---

# Guiding Principle

Every page should have a clear purpose, a defined audience and a logical next action.
