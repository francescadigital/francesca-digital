# Hero Specification

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Hero section is the most important area of the website.

Its only objective is to answer the visitor's first questions immediately and encourage further exploration.

The Hero should never attempt to explain everything.

It should create confidence.

---

# Visitor Questions

Within five seconds the visitor must understand:

1. Who is Francesca Digital?

2. What do they build?

3. Why should I continue reading?

4. What should I do next?

---

# Communication Goal

The Hero should communicate:

- confidence
- precision
- professionalism
- clarity

Never:

- excitement
- hype
- marketing language
- exaggerated claims

---

# Content Structure

The Hero always contains:

Eyebrow

↓

Headline

↓

Supporting paragraph

↓

Primary CTA

↓

Secondary CTA

↓

Trust signals

---

# Eyebrow

Purpose:

Introduce the positioning.

Examples:

Independent Digital Studio

Strategy • Design • Engineering

Digital Product Development

---

# Headline

The headline must satisfy:

- fewer than 8 words
- instantly understandable
- memorable
- timeless

Good examples:

Digital products built with intent.

Build with clarity.

Design for the long term.

Engineering without compromise.

---

# Supporting Paragraph

Length:

2–3 sentences.

Must explain:

Who we help.

What we build.

Why our approach is different.

Never list technologies.

Never mention frameworks.

Focus on outcomes.

---

# Primary CTA

Always action-oriented.

Preferred:

Start a project

Discuss your goals

Book an introduction

---

# Secondary CTA

Always lower emphasis.

Preferred:

View selected work

See our process

Explore services

---

# Trust Signals

Maximum three.

Examples:

Performance-first

Accessible by default

Built for long-term maintenance

---

# Layout Principles

Visual hierarchy must clearly prioritize:

Headline

↓

Supporting paragraph

↓

CTA

↓

Everything else

---

# Motion

Motion supports understanding.

Motion never attracts attention to itself.

Animations should feel:

slow

confident

intentional

---

# Final Principle

The Hero should feel like the beginning of a professional conversation.

Not the beginning of a marketing campaign.
