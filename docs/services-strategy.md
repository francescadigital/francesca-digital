# Services Strategy

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the services offered by Francesca Digital.

It describes what clients actually buy, how services are positioned and how every service should be presented.

Services are outcomes.

Not technologies.

---

# Core Principle

Clients never buy:

React

Next.js

TypeScript

Tailwind CSS

Animations

SEO

Clients buy:

Better communication.

Better conversion.

Better performance.

Better user experience.

Long-term maintainability.

---

# Service Positioning

Every service should answer:

What problem does it solve?

Who is it for?

What is included?

What is the outcome?

---

# Service Structure

Every service follows the same structure.

Title

↓

Problem

↓

Approach

↓

Deliverables

↓

Outcome

↓

CTA

# Primary Services

## Website Design & Development

A professional website built for long-term growth.

## Landing Pages

Purpose

Communicate one idea with clarity.

Outcome

Focused pages designed to convert visitors into clients.

---

## Website Redesign

Purpose

Outcome

Better usability, stronger communication and improved performance.

---

## Technical Improvement

Purpose

Increase quality after launch.

---

# What We Do Not Sell

Cheap websites.

Templates presented as custom work.

One-day delivery.

Unlimited revisions.

SEO guarantees.

Marketing promises.

---

# Communication Principles

Never describe technology first.

Describe business value first.

Technology supports the solution.

It is never the solution itself.

---

# Final Principle

Every service should feel like a professional partnership.

Not a predefined package.
